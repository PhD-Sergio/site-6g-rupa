## Ongoing research

We're currently working on 6G-RUPA applied to Non-Terrestrial Networks, starting with routing in LEO satellite constellations using topological addresses. The [topological forwarding preprint](https://doi.org/10.2139/ssrn.7163182) and the [SNS4SNS poster](/news/20260206-sns4sns-poster-sophia-antipolis/) present the first results. Stay tuned for more updates!
